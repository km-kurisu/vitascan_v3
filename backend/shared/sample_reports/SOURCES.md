# India-Compliant Sample Blood Reports — Sources & Grounding

Downloaded source material lives in `raw_sources/` (`pdfs/` = original PDFs,
`pages/` = HTML snapshots + reconstructed text). The three Scribd documents
(which block plain downloads) were captured with headless Chromium + scroll
events and their full document text reconstructed into
`pages/*_scribd.txt` (Apollo 24|7 full report, Sri CBC+vitamins, Al Nasar
full check-up).

The 6 reports in this folder are **synthetic** (never a real patient) and are
generated by `build_sample_reports.py`. They cover all 9 biomarkers the grader
model consumes: `hemoglobin, mcv, rdw, ferritin, b12, folate, rbc, mch, mchc`.

## Why we built them instead of scraping a real all-in-one report

No single real-world Indian lab report found online carries all 9 biomarkers.
Confirmed by web search: CBC (hemogram) reports include Hb, RBC, MCV, MCH, MCHC,
RDW (and WBC/platelets) but **not** ferritin/B12/folate; those are separate
chemistry/vitamin assays. Indian labs bundle them only as a *panel*:

- Metropolis Healthcare "Anaemia Profile-Nutritional" — CBC + Iron Studies +
  Vitamin B12 + Folic Acid + Ferritin
- Thyrocare "Anemia Profile Basic/Advanced" — Complete Hemogram (24) + ferritin +
  Vitamin B12 + folate
- Ayushman Diagnostics "Anemia Panel" — CBC+PS, Iron Profile, ferritin, Vit B12,
  Folate, TFT, Urea/Creatinine
- Kamal DiagnoCare "Anaemia Profile - I" — CBC, folic acid, serum iron, TIBC,
  reticulocyte, ferritin, Vitamin B12
- Apollo 24|7 "Vitamin & Deficiency Panel" / MycDiagnostics "Nutritional
  Deficiency Plus" (39 parameters) — include CBC indices + ferritin + B12 + folate

So the reports here are **composite panels** assembled from these real formats.

  iron studies + B12 (360 pg/mL) + folate (4.09 ng/mL), ECLIA methods.

## Plausible value grounding

Normal ranges follow common Indian lab intervals:
Hb 13.0–17.0 (M) / 11.5–15.0 (F) g/dL; RBC 4.5–5.5 (M) / 3.8–4.8 (F) M/mm3;
MCV 83–101 fL; MCH 27–32 pg; MCHC 31.5–34.5 g/dL; RDW-CV 11.6–14.0 %;
ferritin 22–322 (M) / 10–120 (F) ng/mL; B12 197–771 pg/mL; folate 3.89–26.8 ng/mL.

Deficient scenarios are anchored to real Indian cohort data — e.g. the RMLIMS
Lucknow study of 160 anaemic antenatal women (IJogr, 2024): mean Hb 8.69 g/dL,
RBC 3.38, MCV 88.23 fL, MCH 27.46 pg, MCHC 31.08 g/dL, RDW 18.75 %, ferritin
49.65 ng/mL, B12 130.21 pg/mL, folate 14.76 ng/mL — plus WHO/Indian cut-offs
(ferritin <15 ng/mL = iron deficiency; MCV >100 = macrocytic; B12 <100 pg/mL
deficient; folate <3.89 ng/mL deficient).

RBC indices are computed, not hand-typed, so they are internally consistent:
`PCV = MCV×RBC/10`, `MCH = Hb×10/RBC`, `MCHC = Hb×100/PCV`.

## Verified end-to-end (2026-09-23)

`run_grader_input_pipeline --pdf-dir backend/shared/sample_reports` resolves
54/54 biomarker values (all reports 9/9). `AnemiaGrader.grade` on the batch
payload returns: 01/02 No Anemia, 03 IDA, 04 Grey Zone Triage (B12 = top class at
0.55), 05 Folate Deficiency, 06 Grey Zone Triage (IDA lean) — matching the
intended clinical scenarios, with the two deliberately "borderline" reports
deferring to triage.
