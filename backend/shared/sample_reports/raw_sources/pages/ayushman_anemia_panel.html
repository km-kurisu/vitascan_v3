    Anemia Panel Test in Dwarka @ ?2499 | Complete Blood Health Checkup

    Are you experiencing fatigue, weakness, or pale skin? Get a comprehensive anemia evaluation with our specialized Anemia Panel Test for just @2499 in Dwarka. This complete diagnostic package helps identify the root cause of anemia and related nutritional deficiencies.

    Anemia  Package Includes:

    • CBC + Peripheral Smear (PS) - Evaluates red blood cells, hemoglobin, and blood cell morphology
    • Iron Profile - Measures iron levels, TIBC, and saturation
    • Ferritin Test - Checks iron stores in your body
    • Vitamin B12 & Folate - Detects vitamin deficiency anemia
    • Thyroid Function Test (TFT) - Rules out thyroid-related anemia
    • Urea & Creatinine - Assesses kidney function (common cause of anemia)

    Why Choose This Package?

    • Affordable at ?2499 - Save 40% compared to individual tests
    • Complete Anemia Workup - Covers all major causes of anemia
    • NABL-Accredited Labs - Reliable and accurate results
    • Fast Reports - Most tests reported within 24 hours
    • Free Doctor Consultation - Expert guidance on your results

    Who Should Get Tested?

    • Individuals experiencing chronic fatigue or weakness
    • Pregnant women (high risk for anemia)
    • Vegetarians/vegans (risk of B12 deficiency)
    • Patients with pale skin or shortness of breath
    • Those with heavy menstrual bleeding
    • People with poor dietary habits
